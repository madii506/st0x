an xStock tracks the share 1:1 but gives no voting rights and no legal claim on the company.

faq

what is st0x?

a terminal for trading tokenized stocks (xStocks) on solana, any hour, any day. live prices, real charts, and trades routed through jupiter that you sign yourself.

is this the real stock?

each xStock is a token backed 1:1 by the underlying share, issued by Backed and held with custodians. it follows the price of the share, but carries no voting rights or legal claim on the company.

what happens on weekends?

on-chain markets keep trading. with no official price coming in, the token can move away from friday's close. when nyse reopens, it usually snaps back toward the real price. the gap section shows this live.

do you hold my money?

no. every trade is a jupiter swap built for your wallet. you approve it in phantom, solflare or backpack, and the tokens land straight in your wallet.

what does it cost?

st0x adds no fee. you pay the solana network fee and the pool's trading fee, which are already inside the quote you see.

who can use it?

xStocks are not offered to US persons and are unavailable in several countries, including the UK, Canada and Australia. you are responsible for following the rules where you live.

which stocks can i trade?

every xStock with real liquidity on solana: the big tech names, banks, consumer brands and funds like SPY and QQQ. the board and the names section show the live list, straight from the chain.

why is the price different from the stock?

the token trades on its own pools. while nyse is open, traders keep the two close. when it's shut, the token prices in news and sentiment the share can't. the gap section shows the difference live.

what are limit orders?

orders that sit on-chain with jupiter and fill only at your price, at any hour, even with this page closed. you can cancel them any time from the terminal.

do alerts work when i close the tab?

no. alerts live in this browser and fire while the page is open. limit orders are the thing that works while you sleep.

what about dividends?

they're reinvested automatically: your xStock balance grows to reflect the payout, net of withholding tax.
